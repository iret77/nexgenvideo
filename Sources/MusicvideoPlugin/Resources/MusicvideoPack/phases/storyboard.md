## Musical and visual construction

Translate approved Script scenes into stable planned shots with explicit adaptation links, visible action, framing and intra-shot moments. Every required Script action must be covered or have its explicitly reviewable adaptation decision. A second moment within a shot is not an editorial cut. Keep world chronology/causality separate from display order. Use native reorder/duplicate/split/delete/timing edits as genuine new canonical revisions; never overwrite a native decision with an older agent draft.

Beat mode places cuts on measured beat/downbeat boundaries when appropriate. Section mode follows the actual musical structure and can use several atomic coverage shots within a section. Multicam uses two to five distinct camera setups across the complete song, each beginning at zero and ending at the exact original audio end. Do not invent section timestamps, shorten the song to a frame boundary, or duplicate a shot identity merely because it overlaps two musical sections.

Give each shot one readable primary action, concrete start/end state and a deliberate camera/framing intent. Vary shot size, axis and height with purpose; consecutive shots of one subject need an intentional size or angle change, or an explicit jump-cut intent. Establish a new location unless deliberately withholding orientation. Resolve figure entrances/exits, eyelines, screen direction, positions, prop ownership, floor contact and visible world zones. Additional required image moments make changes inspectable inside a long shot.

Preserve the Brief's visual language and selected director pattern; justify a deliberate departure in the canonical intent. Refrains may repeat a motif while changing its state or visual function. Avoid unmotivated framing/camera monotony and unplanned crowds, off-screen actors in visible descriptions or literal model instructions phrased as metaphors. Plan demanded identities, location views, props, state variants and geometry for later Shot Planning/Bible without inventing existing assets. Use actual model-reference capabilities to expose infeasible obligations early; do not silently drop a required reference.

Before requesting approve_gate for storyboard, show the genuine sketches at useful size with the corresponding action/framing and exact timing. Summarize the demand overview and visual/musical arc. Text-only descriptions, placeholder rectangles, compile tokens and pending generation tasks do not complete the Storyboard.
