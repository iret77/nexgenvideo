## Musical pacing and executable shot craft

Use the measured perceived BPM as an editorial guide: 120+ BPM usually suggests 1–2 s average shots with a 4 s tempo cap; 90–120 BPM 2–4 s/6 s; 60–90 BPM 3–5 s/8 s; below 60 BPM 5–8 s/12 s. These are editorial pacing guides, separate from spend and the selected route's executable duration limits. Multicam source plans are exempt from these short-cut tempo caps: every camera setup retains the full song from zero to its exact end. Do not split or shorten those source plans to satisfy an editorial pacing guide; cutting between camera setups happens in the later edit. Deliberate stillness or an extended continuous performance needs an explicit canonical rationale. Do not invent extra action to fill duration. If the approved timing is unsuitable, rewind Storyboard and revise coverage rather than changing it silently during promotion.

Preserve camera axis, lens/framing, physical scale, eyelines, entrances/exits, prop ownership and concrete start/end states. A generated action stays atomic and physically readable. Write provider-facing content in English as concrete visible instructions, with positive constraints and no accidental metaphor, unbound group or off-frame performer. Text/title cards require the approved Brief decision. Semantic actors and reference roles come from the actual graph; provider-specific upload tags/order are emitted by the compiler, never invented in prose.

Resolve model/offering duration, aspect, resolution, real first/last-frame and reference policies, audio inputs/output and extension operation from the live executable catalog. A keyframe-driven route and a reference-driven route have different conditioning; never send a first frame through a model's identity/style slot or silently remove required references to fit its cap. If moderation risk changes the intended content or delivery mode, expose the proposed change and require the appropriate native edit/rewind. Animated still is an explicit delivery choice with real timeline motion, not a hidden fallback for failed video generation.

Display the promoted shots in canonical order with source/range, intended route, planned versus deferred inputs, exact obligations, musical performance and coverage summary. Request approve_gate for shotlist only after actual assets, receipts and typed extension validate. Approval does not prove future motion, singing, lip sync or final image quality.
