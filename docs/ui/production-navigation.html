Timeline

Spans the full width below the cockpit and the right column. Tasks remains full height.

  • Project Brief and Treatment are separate documents with separate scroll areas and retained unsaved structured Brief edits across tab switches.
  • Storyboard is a normal cockpit tab. No separate review sheet, modal Done button or alternate content renderer.
  • A pending artifact review marks its destination tab with an accessible new-content indication. Clicking that tab, navigating to it from the review card, approval or decline clears it. A new request may mark it again; a rewind decision never marks an artifact as new.
  • Pipeline alone shows overall phase progress. Every pending input points to Tasks, where its actual controls live. Reading a Bible card never turns it into the current workflow step.
  • Bible cards prioritize reference images and identity. Preview shows the selected image; Inspector owns detailed properties. Film Preview can be restored without altering the timeline.
  • Approval is bound to the offered artifact bytes and lineage. A newer Shot List supersedes the previous version. Changed content requires a refreshed request.
  • Revising previously approved work has a separate “Revise from …” decision naming affected approvals and the reason. Decline preserves approvals. Retrying a request after reconnect reuses the same decision.

Native acceptance
